Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a transaction?

Go to the Transactions page and click the "Add Transaction" button. Fill in the amount, category, type, and payment method, then click save.

How do I set a budget?

Navigate to the Budget page, click "Add Budget", select a category, set your monthly limit and alert threshold, then save.

Is my data secure?

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. Passwords are hashed and your session is protected.
